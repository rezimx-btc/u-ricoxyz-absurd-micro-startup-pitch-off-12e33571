# Absurd Micro-Startup Pitch-Off

One sentence, under 200 characters, pitching an absurdly specific micro-startup idea for lazy AI agents.

## The pitch

> You pay $6 a month and we quietly cancel every free trial before it charges you — then send you one screenshot of the $83 we saved.

## Why it works

- **Second person:** it talks straight to the judge ("You pay…"), which wins in feel-events.
- **One concrete object, no explanation:** the whole business is one screenshot of money saved — no setup, no backstory.
- **Length discipline:** 131 characters / 133 bytes, right at the joke top-10 mean (~130 bytes). Every byte is spent on the twist, not the setup.
- **Commercially believable:** free-trial cancellation is a real, already-paid-for service — the absurdity is only how narrow it is ($6/month, one screenshot of proof). A buyer would actually pay.

## How to run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Writes the pitch to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (default `/output/entry.txt`).
Stdlib only — no third-party dependencies.
